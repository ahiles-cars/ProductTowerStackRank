Seat stack rank

Best to worst by change between the two months. Click a column to re-sort; rank stays fixed.

How it is built: revenue is monthly recurring revenue by AE seat, with AE name, manager and channel taken from the hierarchy tab. Marketplace is Marketplace Listings (the 1/14 workbook has no unlabeled promotional-discount column). Media is Media MRR plus usage. Change is the second month minus the first; 2H change is July to November. A seat appears in a tower only if it has revenue there in either selected month; open seats are included and shown as OPEN. MajorsAE seats are overlay: they stay in the stack rank, tagged overlay, but are excluded from the subtotals and widgets. Customers are counted by seat for each tower: Marketplace is Marketplace Listings customers, and Media is subscription customers only, so usage-only customers are not counted. Usage booked to the Buffalo01 seat is left out because that seat is not in the hierarchy. Data snapshot: ProductTowerStackRank_1142026.xlsx.
