Seat stack rank

Best to worst by change between the two months. Click a column to re-sort; rank stays fixed.

How it is built: revenue is monthly recurring revenue by AE seat, with AE name, manager and channel taken from the hierarchy tab. Marketplace is Marketplace Listings. Media is Media MRR only: the UsageActuals tab in the 11/9 workbook is an exact copy of MRRActuals, so adding it would double count and usage is left out. Media revenue here is therefore lower than in the 11/5 version, which included usage, and Media is not comparable to it until a corrected usage tab is supplied. Change is the second month minus the first; 2H change is July to November. A seat appears in a tower only if it has revenue there in either selected month; open seats are included and shown as OPEN. MajorsAE seats are overlay: they stay in the stack rank, tagged overlay, but are excluded from the subtotals and widgets. Customers are counted by seat for each tower: Marketplace is Marketplace Listings customers, and Media is subscription customers only, so usage-only customers are not counted.
